About DEESU Restaurant

DEESU Restaurant was founded in 2016 with a simple goal: to serve high-quality meals with consistent taste, exceptional service, and a welcoming dining experience. What started as a single restaurant has grown into a trusted food brand serving thousands of customers every month across Pakistan.

Our Mission

About DEESU

DEESU is a small team of engineers who design, build, and operate production software for growing businesses — Android apps, web platforms, EdTech and LMS systems, UI/UX design, technical documentation, and the cloud infrastructure underneath.

Our Story

Founded in 2019, DEESU started by taking on Android and web development work directly — no layer between the person writing the proposal and the person writing the code.

That's stayed true as the practice grew to cover EdTech and LMS systems under our D.TEN division, UI/UX design, technical documentation, and the cloud infrastructure that keeps all of it running.

We don't measure a project by the handoff. Founders, universities, and enterprise teams work with us because someone answers when a system needs attention months after launch.
